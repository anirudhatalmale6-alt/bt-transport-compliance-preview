For Beavan and his academy partner · 16 September 2026 · Separate venture, separate Microsoft account, nothing to do with BT Transport Management.

1. What I understand you want — correct me if this is wrong

A training and compliance system of your own, doing the same job as the GXO one you showed me. Your trainers sign in; the learners never do. A trainer records the session, both parties sign on the tablet or the PC, and the system produces a signed PDF that gets emailed to the client company. Every person has their own folder holding everything they have ever signed.

If that is not it, say so before you pay for anything.

2. Two decisions already made, and what they save you

Learners do not sign in. Only you and your trainers. That means no Microsoft licence per learner — you can train a hundred drivers for another firm and it costs you nothing extra in subscriptions. This is what makes the whole thing affordable to run, and it is why SharePoint and Power Apps is the right foundation.

One app, not two. GXO run a desktop version and a tablet version. You do not need both. One app works on both, exactly as your fleet system already does. Two apps would mean every change made twice, and eventually somebody signs the wrong version.

7. The one piece with real technical risk

Turning the signed document into a PDF automatically, and getting the signature image out of the tablet and into SharePoint, both need a background flow. I have built working flows for you before, but these are the fiddliest parts of the job, and automatic PDF conversion may need a paid Microsoft add-on on top of your subscription.

I will prove both work before you pay a penny for modules 4 and 5. If the PDF needs a paid add-on, I will tell you exactly what it costs and you decide. If it cannot be done cleanly, you are not charged for it.

8. Copyright — you raised this first, and you were right to

  • Fine: how the system works. Screens, features, workflow, the idea of a training record with traffic lights. You cannot copyright the way software functions.
  • Not fine: GXO or B&Q branding, and their actual SSOW and handbook content. That is theirs.
  • Worth knowing: the medical conditions list and the 20-metre eyesight standard come from DVLA rules, not from GXO. The substance is public. We write our own wording and you are on solid ground.
  • I am not a lawyer and this is not legal advice. If your partner is bringing material from a previous employer, get it checked before it goes near the system.
